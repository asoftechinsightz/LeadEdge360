# Workstream 8 — Business Growth Playbook (MSME)

**Program:** LeadEdge360 AI Growth Engine Phase-1  
**Audience:** Non-technical MSME owners (Tenant #1 style)  
**Date:** August 2026

> **Executive growth guide:** `docs/customer-success/AI_BUSINESS_GROWTH_PLAYBOOK.md` — consolidated MSME narrative. This document remains the **step-by-step UI walkthrough**.

---

## How to use this playbook

One section per business goal. Each step uses **only** what LeadEdge360 already provides. No technical setup beyond sign-in.

---

## 1. First login experience

1. Go to `https://app.asoftechinsightz.com/signin`  
2. Sign in with work email or Google  
3. Accept DPDP consent if prompted  
4. You land on **Dashboard** — workspace overview  
5. See **LeadEdge360** product card → open CRM anytime  

**Tip:** Bookmark `/dashboard` and `/leadedge360`.

---

## 2. Complete your business profile

1. On Dashboard, scroll to **AI GROWTH ENGINE**  
2. Click **Improve profile**  
3. Fill: business name, category, phone, website, hours  
4. Select **service areas** (cities you serve)  
5. Add **keywords** (what customers search for)  
6. Turn on **WhatsApp messaging configured** when ready  
7. Add **5 FAQs** (questions customers ask you daily)  
8. Watch **Business Completeness** rise toward 80%+  

**GST / PAN / logo:** Keep in your records; add logo on Google Business Profile separately.

---

## 3. Find new customers

1. Open **LeadEdge360** → **+ New lead** or use website/WhatsApp ingest  
2. Check **SOURCES** chart — which channels work  
3. Read **Growth scanner** tips on Dashboard  
4. Add leads from Google and WhatsApp (highest intent in India)  
5. Assign territories so **Local Visibility** KPI improves  

---

## 4. Run WhatsApp campaigns

1. In lead table, click **WhatsApp** icon on a lead  
2. Edit the pre-filled message before sending  
3. For bulk nurture, ask CS to enable n8n WhatsApp automation (pilot)  
4. Use **WhatsApp outreach** AI draft (if enabled) → copy → send manually  
5. Only message leads who opted in (`WhatsApp opt-in` on lead)

---

## 5. Improve local search presence

1. Complete website + service areas in profile  
2. Add FAQs like “Do you serve [city]?” and “What are your prices?”  
3. Track **Local Visibility** % on Dashboard  
4. Use **GBP post draft** → paste into Google Business Profile  
5. Aim for at least one **Google** lead in your pipeline (checklist item)

---

## 6. Increase customer reviews

1. In profile, enter **review count**, **average rating**, **pending replies**  
2. Watch **Review Health** on Dashboard  
3. Use review reply AI draft → **verify** → post on Google manually  
4. Create follow-up tasks for each pending reply (CS can help with n8n)

---

## 7. Track your sales pipeline

1. **LeadEdge360** table = your pipeline  
2. Status flow: New → Contacted → Qualified → Proposal → Won / Lost  
3. Dashboard shows **Total leads**, **Hot leads**, **Conversion**  
4. Click a lead row for **AI score** and reasons  
5. Filter by territory and status  

---

## 8. Improve proposal conversion

1. Move serious leads to **Qualified** then **Proposal**  
2. Re-score after updating lead message with budget/timeline  
3. Focus on **Hot** label leads first  
4. Weekly: count leads stuck in Proposal &gt; 14 days — re-contact via WhatsApp  

---

## 9. Follow-up automation

1. Respond to hot leads within 24 hours (manual)  
2. CS enables n8n: stale New leads get WhatsApp template (opt-in only)  
3. Google-source leads prioritized in automation  
4. AEO reminders (profile, FAQ, reviews) appear as follow-up tasks when configured  

---

## 10. Weekly growth routine (30 minutes)

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| Monday | Record AEO Score + top 3 recommendations |
| Tuesday | Add or improve 1 FAQ |
| Wednesday | Contact all hot leads |
| Thursday | Fix cold leads (company name, territory) |
| Friday | Review conversion % vs last week |

---

## 11. Monthly business review

1. Screenshot Dashboard KPIs + AEO row  
2. Compare: leads, hot count, conversion %, AEO Score  
3. List territories with zero leads — marketing focus next month  
4. Review with Customer Success using [06_CUSTOMER_SUCCESS_ENABLEMENT.md](./06_CUSTOMER_SUCCESS_ENABLEMENT.md) checklist  
5. Set 3 goals for next month (leads, conversion, completeness)

---

## Support

- Product questions: Customer Success  
- Login / billing: support channel per tenant contract  
- **Do not** expect Invoice, Customer 360, or full ERP in this pilot — leads + growth focus only
